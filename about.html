Light it with care

A flame is a small guest in your home. A few kind habits keep every evening warm and safe.

  • Never leave a burning candle unattended.
  • Place it on a heat-resistant surface, away from curtains and drafts.
  • Keep it away from children and pets.
  • Trim the wick before relighting for a clean, even flame.
  • Our Mithai candles are decorative only and are not edible.
