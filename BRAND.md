# Tohfa & Twinkle: brand notes

Derived from `Tohfa and Twinkle Catalogue Diwali 26.pdf`.

## Idea
Tohfa means gift. Twinkle is the little glow it brings. The brand sells candles, floaters, aroma sets and festive decor as gifts that make an occasion, or a quiet evening, feel held.

Audience: affluent families gifting at festivals and weddings; women in their 30s who treat themselves and their homes (desks, bedrooms, quiet corners).

## Mark
Wordmark in wide-tracked caps, led by a gold four-point star (the "twinkle"). The star is also the bullet, the divider ornament and the tier rating, so one symbol carries the whole system. The star twinkles gently in the nav (reduced-motion safe).

## Palette (sampled from the catalogue)
| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Wine maroon | `#5C1F2D` | brand ground: hero, bands, footer |
| Deep maroon | `#3A1019` | text, button text on gold |
| Antique gold | `#C9A45C` | the single accent: lines, stars, primary button |
| Cream | `#F8EEDF` / `#F0E0C8` | page and alternate sections |
| Gold ink | `#86601D` | small gold text on cream (4.9:1 contrast) |

Dark mode swaps the page to deep wine and the text to cream. Maroon bands and gold stay the same.

## Type
Cormorant Garamond (display, italic for emphasis) with Hanken Grotesk (body, tracked caps for labels). A classical serif is justified here: the catalogue itself is set in one and the brand is festive heritage.

## Shape language
Everything is square-edged except the arch, taken from the catalogue cover (jharokha window). Arches carry product photography, with an offset gold outline. No pills or capsules anywhere.

## Voice
Warm, plain and affectionate. Short sentences. Gifts "glow", evenings are "held". Mithai candles are always flagged as for lighting only. No claims beyond the catalogue.
