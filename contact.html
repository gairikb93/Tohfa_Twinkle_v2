Good to know

Can I add a handwritten note?

Yes. Add one to any Love & Bloom gift by sharing your message when you order.

Can you put together a hamper?

Of course. Pair a Matki or Phool set with a Mithai box, then message us and we will help you build it.

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

We do. Message us for bulk festive orders, return gifts and corporate gifting.

Are the mithai candles edible?

No. They look good enough to eat, but they are strictly for lighting. Please keep them away from children and pets.

How are prices shown?

All prices are per box, as listed in the shop. We confirm your order and delivery details once you message us.
